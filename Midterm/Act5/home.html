Find the latest gaming Desktop, graphics cards and more at unbeatable specs. Build your dream PC with our easy-to-use custom PC builder. Choose high-performance components for gaming, work, or everyday.

About

A PC builder is designed to guide users through the process of creating a custom PC by selecting components such as the CPU, motherboard, RAM, graphics card, storage, and power supply while ensuring compatibility between parts. These tools are especially useful for beginners or enthusiasts who want to build a PC tailored to their needs, whether for gaming, content creation, or general use

Programs

  1. BuildCores – Offers a 3D PC builder and part picker with over 3,000 3D models. It allows users to check compatibility, compare prices, and visualize their build before purchase.
  2. CORSAIR PC Builder – A step-by-step configurator that ensures compatibility for all components, suitable for both high-end and budget builds
  3. SI EZ PC Builder – An AI-powered assistant that provides personalized recommendations based on your needs, such as gaming resolution, GPU choice, or aesthetic preferences. It ensures all selected parts are compatible and optimized for performance
  4. Resolvebit PC Builder – Offers automatic compatibility checking, real-time feedback on power requirements, and estimated FPS for games at 1080p, 1440p, and 4K. It also provides suitability scores for gaming, streaming, and productivity tasks
